## Buying Guide

### What should I check before buying indoor paddleboard storage?

Your primary concern is stud spacing and hardware quality. Most heavy-duty SUP racks are designed to anchor directly into wooden wall studs. If your garage studs are spaced at 16 or 24 inches, ensure the rack's base plates accommodate that spread. Relying on drywall anchors for a 35-pound paddleboard is a guaranteed recipe for a collapsed rack. 

Always check the included mounting hardware. A recurring pattern across budget racks is the inclusion of cheap, brittle screws that shear off inside the wall. Replacing stock screws with high-quality structural screws or lag bolts before installation is a simple way to prevent sudden failures. Also, verify that the rack's arms are long enough for your board's width; standard touring boards sit around 30 to 32 inches wide.

### How much water capacity should I carry?

When setting up your gear room for indoor paddleboard storage for outdoor recreation, you will likely store your day-trip supplies nearby. Whether you are prepping for a coastal paddle or planning a remote hike-in route, carrying the right water capacity is a safety requirement. For a full day of paddling or hiking under the sun, plan for at least 3 to 4 liters of water per person. Storing your hiking hydration reservoirs and backcountry water filters on the shelves right below your wall-mounted boards makes it easy to grab your entire kit before heading out. Make sure you also pack appropriate [pants for hiking hydration](/outdoor-recreation/best-pants/) and [paddling jackets](/outdoor-recreation/best-jackets/) if you are portaging through dense brush.

### What hydration complaints matter most?

The fastest way to ruin your indoor gear storage area is a slow leak from a poorly stored hydration bladder. When buyers evaluate outdoor water systems, leak prevention is the loudest complaint. If a bite valve or reservoir seam fails on the shelf, it can drip down and pool on your [paddleboard bags](/outdoor-recreation/best-paddleboard-bags/) or [paddleboard accessories](/outdoor-recreation/best-paddleboard-accessories/). To avoid this, always empty and air-dry your backcountry water bladders completely before hanging them near your paddleboard storage racks.
